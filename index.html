Our products

Behind every box is Þorbjörn, a family-owned fishing company with deep roots in Grindavík.

Thorfish brings together Icelandic seafood and generations of fishing experience. Our fish is wild-caught in the waters around Iceland and processed and frozen at sea aboard our freezer trawler, Hrafn Sveinbjarnarson.

From handling the catch to preparing and packing the finished product, our crew’s experience makes the difference.

Wild-caught Icelandic species

Frozen-at-sea fillets and whole fish, in a range of sizes and presentations.

01

Cod

Gadus morhua

With its firm white flesh, large flakes and mild flavour, Icelandic cod is a dependable favourite in kitchens around the world. Our frozen-at-sea cod fillets are available in a range of size grades and specifications to suit different markets and customer requirements.

02

Haddock

Melanogrammus aeglefinus

Haddock is valued for its fine white flesh, delicate flavour and tender flakes. A traditional favourite for fish and chips, it is also well suited to smoking and a wide range of other preparations. Our haddock fillets are processed and frozen aboard Hrafn Sveinbjarnarson, with different sizes and presentations available.

03

Saithe

Pollachius virens

Saithe is a versatile whitefish with a firm texture and a fuller flavour. Its flesh becomes pale when cooked and holds together well, making it suitable for battered portions, prepared meals and further processing.

04

Redfish

Sebastes norvegicus

Redfish offers a delicate, slightly sweet flavour and firm, succulent flesh. Its distinctive red skin and versatility make it a valued product across a range of international markets.

05

Greenland halibut

Reinhardtius hippoglossoides

Greenland halibut is prized for its rich flavour and naturally high oil content, which gives the flesh a soft, succulent texture. It is particularly well suited to smoking, grilling and a variety of traditional preparations.
